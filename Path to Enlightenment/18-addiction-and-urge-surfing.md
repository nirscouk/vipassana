# Chapter 18 · Addiction: food, drink, smoke, and the phone

The same law that works on a pain zone works on junk food, alcohol, cigarettes, vaping, gambling, and the restless hand that opens a screen. Addiction is not first a philosophy. It is the mind reacting blindly to an uncomfortable sensation and calling the reaction relief.

A chemical or a “bliss point” of sugar, salt, and fat can hit the reward system hard. You think the drink relaxes you. Often it only silences the withdrawal the last dose created. When the level in the blood drops, the body alarms: a band around the chest, an empty ache in the stomach, sweat, heat, a jitter in the nerves. The untrained mind reads that alarm as an emergency and consumes. That is *rāga* — craving — wearing a familiar costume.

Willpower fights the *thought* of the substance and wears out. Vipassanā does not fight the thought. It sits with the *sensation* of the urge until the wave falls.

### How a junk-food craving is built

A picture, a stress, a bored evening. The brain remembers the hit. Then the craving becomes physical: hollow stomach, tight throat, restlessness in the chest, saliva. That discomfort is not yet hunger that needs a meal. It is a wave. If you eat to make the wave stop, you teach the wave to return.

### What to do when the hand is already moving

1. **Pause.** Do not open the cupboard. Do not light it. Do not unlock the phone. One word is enough: *stop*.
2. **Locate.** Close the eyes for half a minute if you can. Leave the picture of the food or the drink. Where does the urge live — vibration in the chest, contraction in the belly, restlessness in the fingers?
3. **Stay without war.** Do not say “I should not want this.” That is aversion, another fuel. Look as a scientist looks: pressure, heat, pulse.
4. **Know *anicca*.** Arisen. It will pass. Watch it change shape or lose voltage.

Many chemical-emotional surges last about ninety seconds if you do not restoke them with thought. Sit through that minute and a half. Each time you do, the old pathway *discomfort means consume* is not carved deeper. That is extinction, not heroism.

### Alcohol, nicotine, and the screen

Map the withdrawal as you would map pain.

- **Cigarettes and vaping** often sit as tightness in the chest, a dry throat, fidget in fingers and jaw.
- **Alcohol** often sits as a hollow churn in the stomach, a faster heart, cloudy pressure in the head.
- **Phones and gambling** often sit as a high, jittery vibration in the upper body and hands that will not stay.

Drop the story: “I need this to relax; I have earned it; just one.” The story is smoke. The sensation is the fire. Anchor at the centre of the discomfort. Is the tightness static, or does it throb? Is the anxiety moving? Because it changes, it can end.

An addictive peak, if you do not feed it, often lasts less than five to ten minutes. Let it wash over the mountain. You are the mountain, not the storm.

If two habits are chained — smoke only with a drink, scroll only in bed — see the pair. Break the first link with the same pause. If the body is in dangerous withdrawal from alcohol or another drug, get medical help. Equanimity is not a substitute for a doctor. It is how you meet what the doctor cannot sit through for you.

**Today.** Name your strongest regular urge — sugar, drink, smoke, or the phone. The next time it arrives, do not argue with it. Find it in the body. Stay ninety seconds. If you act anyway, do not add shame. Note where you left the wave, and begin again next time.
