# A letter to the one who is starting

> Appamādena sampādetha — strive on with diligence.
>
> — The Buddha’s last words

Friend,

You do not need to be ready. Readiness is often another delay. You need a precept you will keep today, a seat you will return to tomorrow, and a willingness to see what you would rather improve or escape.

The Buddha walked this path with a human body and a human mind. He did not hoard the way. He left it in the open: know suffering, drop its cause, taste its end, develop the eight limbs. Sit. Live cleanly. Love without devouring. Serve. Begin again.

Accept. Do not ignore. Do not give importance. Everything is changing.

The path is awareness — subtle, then more subtle. Walking is enough. You may walk.

**Sabbe sattā sukhitā hontu**  
*May all beings be happy.*
